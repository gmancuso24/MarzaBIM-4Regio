# MarzaBIM-4Regio

**IFC models of the domestic architecture of Regio IV, ancient Kainua (Marzabotto, Bologna, Italy).**

---

## Archaeological context

Regio IV is one of the main residential quarters of ancient Kainua, the Etruscan city at Marzabotto. This repository collects IFC-native BIM reconstructions of the excavated houses of Regio IV, organised by insula (urban block).

---

## Repository structure

The repository is organised into one folder per insula:

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| `1_insula/` | Houses 1–6 of Insula 1, Regio IV ("Isolato Mansuelli"), excavated 1952–1961 (P.E. Arias, G.A. Mansuelli). Modelled as a whole in two chronological phases (`RIV-I1_F1.ifc`, `RIV-I1_F2.ifc`). |
| `2_insula/` | Casa 1 of Insula 2, Regio IV, excavated by the University of Bologna (1988–1998). Modelled in four construction phases (`C1_F1.ifc`–`C1_F4.ifc`). |

Each folder contains its own `readme.md` with detailed archaeological context, phasing, and reference publications specific to that insula.
